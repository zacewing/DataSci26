How plateau detection works

Every week, a straight line is fit through the last 6 weeks of sessions. Its slope is the rate of progress. Gaining under 0.5% of the lift per week is flagged as a plateau. These settings were tuned on 80 simulated lifters and tested on 200 others: F1 56%, better than the simple rule "no PR in 3 weeks" (52%), at the cost of some false alarms. See Results.
